# DrapeLoop — User Scenarios

Updated: 2026-10-18

## Scenario 1: Creating a personal profile for the first time

Han sees a top online and wants to preview how it might look on him. He opens the product for the first time, follows the photo guidance, uploads a full-body photo, and enters his height and weight. He does not need to know his chest, waist, or hip measurements.

The system checks the photo against basic requirements. Han confirms the photo, completes his first try-on, checks whether the person still looks like him, and saves his personal profile for future use.

## Scenario 2: Trying another outfit without another photo

A few days later, Han finds two more tops. He opens the product, uses his saved profile, uploads the two clothing images separately, and generates results to compare. He does not need to take or upload another personal photo.

## Scenario 3: A visibly distorted result

Han notices that a result has made his waist slimmer. He compares it with the original and reports inaccurate body proportions. He can replace his photo or optionally provide a side-view photo when that feature has been validated and enabled. The system does not force him to enter body measurements or describe the distorted result as an accurate representation of fit.
